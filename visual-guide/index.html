5 · Layers and masks

Stack glass sheets. Paint on one without touching the others.

  1. 1Paint layer: normal pixels (the blob)
  2. 2Group: a folder of layers ("Character")
  3. 3Vector layer: shapes and text ("Dear stranger,")
  4. 4Filter layer: blurs everything below it (soft background)
  5. 5Fill layer: a flat colour, here a sunset tint
  6. 6Clone layer: a live copy (the twin blob)
  7. 7File layer: a picture file linked from disk (the star)

Masks: change a layer without painting on it
